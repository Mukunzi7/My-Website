70Scholar

Startup pitch deck

There’s a version of your future still waiting to be found.

A discovery, builder, funding, and marketplace platform for students and young builders.

02

Problem

Opportunities exist. The right students do not find them in time.

Scholarships, internships, grants, and competitions are scattered across the internet. Even when students find them, they often lack a workspace to build the project, portfolio, or pitch required to win.

03

Why now

1

Youth need better pathways into work and funding.

2

Remote opportunities and global competitions are growing.

3

Student-built software, AI tools, and prototypes are becoming valuable proof.

04

Solution

Discover. Build. Launch. Fund.

  1. Find matched opportunities.
  2. Save deadlines and applications.
  3. Build projects in a private workspace.
  4. Publish to a marketplace for funding, mentorship, or sale.
05

Product

The MVP is already working.

70Scholar now includes authentication, a dashboard, opportunity finder, builder workspace, file uploads, project marketplace, pledge funding, admin dashboard, automatic imports, and payment-ready checkout.
