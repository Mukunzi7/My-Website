# EduBridge Startup Pitch Deck

## 1. Title

**EduBridge**  
There's a version of your future still waiting to be found.

A discovery, builder, funding, and marketplace platform for students and young builders.

## 2. Problem

Students miss scholarships, internships, grants, competitions, and early career opportunities because information is scattered, deadlines are hidden, and generic lists do not match their real level.

Even when students find an opportunity, they often lack a place to build a strong project, portfolio, pitch, or application before the deadline.

## 3. Why Now

- Youth unemployment and underemployment remain major challenges across Africa.
- More scholarships, hackathons, and youth grants are online than ever.
- AI, no-code tools, and remote work make student-built projects more valuable.
- Funders increasingly want proof of initiative, not just grades.

## 4. Solution

EduBridge helps students:

1. Discover real opportunities matched to level, field, country, and deadline.
2. Save and track opportunities inside an account.
3. Build projects in a private workspace.
4. Upload prototypes, apps, files, and links.
5. Publish projects to a marketplace for funding, mentorship, or sale.

## 5. Product

Current MVP includes:

- Landing page
- Authentication
- Opportunity directory
- Student dashboard
- Builder workspace
- File uploads
- Project marketplace
- Fundraising pledge system
- Admin dashboard
- Automatic opportunity RSS import
- Payment-ready integration points

## 6. Target Users

Primary users:

- Secondary school students
- University students
- Recent graduates
- Young developers and creators
- Competition/hackathon teams

Secondary users:

- Funders
- NGOs
- Schools
- Mentors
- Buyers of student-built software/apps

## 7. Market Opportunity

EduBridge starts with African students and expands globally.

Initial beachhead:

- Rwanda and East Africa
- STEM students
- Scholarship and competition applicants
- Youth-led innovation projects

Expansion:

- Pan-African opportunity discovery
- School partnerships
- NGO/funder project pipelines
- Student software marketplace

## 8. Business Model

Potential revenue streams:

1. Freemium student accounts
2. Premium application/project builder tools
3. Marketplace transaction commission
4. Featured opportunity listings from organizations
5. School and university subscriptions
6. Funder dashboards for scouting projects
7. Mentorship and review services

## 9. Competitive Advantage

EduBridge is not only an opportunity list.

It combines:

- Discovery
- Project building
- Progress saving
- Funding
- Selling
- Portfolio proof

This creates a stronger loop: students find opportunities, build for them, publish their work, attract support, and improve their next application.

## 10. Go-To-Market

Phase 1: Rwanda student builders

- Partner with schools, coding clubs, and university societies.
- Run monthly opportunity newsletters.
- Host project-building challenges.
- Feature top student projects publicly.

Phase 2: Funders and NGOs

- Invite small funders to sponsor categories.
- Build dashboards for youth project discovery.
- Partner with education nonprofits.

Phase 3: Regional expansion

- East Africa first, then pan-African coverage.

## 11. Impact

EduBridge can help students:

- Find scholarships before deadlines.
- Build stronger portfolios.
- Turn assignments into real products.
- Get funding for early prototypes.
- Sell small apps, programs, and digital tools.
- Become visible to funders and mentors.
